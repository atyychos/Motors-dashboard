# Bike Sales Dashboard | Customer Analytics & Business Intelligence Project
  
## Project Overview 
This project presents an end-to-end business analytics solution built using Microsoft Excel to analyze customer demographics, income patterns, commute behavior, and bike purchase decisions.

The objective is to transform raw customer data into meaningful insights that help understand purchasing behavior and identify key drivers influencing bike purchases.

The interactive dashboard enables stakeholders to explore data across multiple dimensions such as gender, age, education, region, marital status, and commute distance.

---

## Business Objectives 

- Analyze factors influencing bike purchase decisions
- Understand customer demographics and income distribution
- Identify patterns based on age groups and commute distance
- Compare purchasing behavior across gender, education, and marital status
- Evaluate regional differences in bike buying behavior

## Project Workflow

### 1️) Data Import
- Imported raw customer dataset into Microsoft Excel
- Verified structure and data consistency

---

### 2️) Data Cleaning
Performed data preprocessing to ensure accuracy:

- Handled missing values
- Standardized categorical values (Gender, Region, Education)
- Corrected data types for numerical and date fields
- Ensured consistency across all attributes

---

### 3️) Data Preparation & Feature Understanding
No heavy feature engineering required; focus was placed on:

- Segmenting age into brackets (Juvenile, Middle Age, Old)
- Grouping commute distances for behavioral analysis
- Structuring data for Pivot Table analysis

---

### 4️) Dashboard Development
Built an interactive Excel dashboard using Pivot Tables and Charts.

## Key Business Insights

- Middle-aged customers are the highest bike purchasers
- Customers with shorter commute distances are more likely to purchase bikes
- Male customers show slightly higher purchase volume than female customers
- Higher income groups demonstrate stronger purchase probability
- Graduates and bachelors represent the strongest customer segments

---

## Business Recommendations

- Target marketing campaigns toward middle-aged professionals
- Promote cycling benefits for short-distance commuters
- Focus advertising on high-income customer segments
- Develop region-specific promotional strategies
- Offer educational institution-based awareness campaigns
